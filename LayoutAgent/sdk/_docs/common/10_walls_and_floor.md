**Rules for walls**

0. Room-shape terms (square, rectangle, L-shape, U-shape, T-shape, and the Chinese 方形/L形/U形/T形) always describe a CLOSED room. A "U-shaped room" means a fully-enclosed room whose boundary outline traces a U (two parallel legs plus a connecting bar): create wall segments on every side to close it. Never reduce a shape to 3 open walls.
1. Ensure the walls always enclose the room completely, with no openings.
2. The local +Z (front) direction of each wall must point toward the interior of the room. A direction counts as interior if it contains the embedded floor.

The floor must follow the wall boundary with no unnecessary overhangs. It may be assembled from rectangles.
